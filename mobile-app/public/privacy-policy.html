GUIDE VOYAGER · VIJAYAWADA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains how the Guide Voyager mobile app and website handle information. Guide Voyager is an offline-first guide to places and services in Vijayawada.

Information the app handles

  • Account information: when you register, the service stores your email address, a salted password hash, account role and account creation time. Passwords are not stored in readable form.
  • App activity (on by default): Guide Voyager sends pages opened and actions such as searches, saved places, day plans and requests for directions. Search words, passwords, QR contents and exact location are not included. Account activity is linked to your account. Guest activity uses a random identifier stored on that phone. Activity waits on the phone while offline and is sent when internet returns. You can turn sharing off in Settings; this stops future sharing and clears activity waiting to send.
  • Feedback: place ratings and any note you choose to write are saved on your device until sent, then stored on the service for review. Feedback submitted while signed in is associated with that account; guest feedback is associated with a random guest identifier.
  • Location: if you choose to grant location permission, the app uses the device location on your device to calculate an offline route. The app does not send location coordinates to Guide Voyager’s server.
  • Offline preferences: saved places, itineraries, language, text size and map start-point choices are stored locally on your device. They are not synced to an account.

How information is used and shared

Account details are used to sign you in and associate activity or feedback with your account. App activity is shared by default and helps the Guide Voyager administrator understand how the app is used and improve place information. You can stop sharing in Settings. Feedback you choose to send is handled separately. Data is hosted by Render as the application and database hosting provider. Guide Voyager does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

The app does not include live map tiles. Its bundled offline map is used on the device. Directions links, when opened, go to the selected external mapping service.

Retention and deletion

App activity sent to the service is automatically removed after 24 months. Account deletion removes the account, account-linked analytics, account-linked feedback and server-side saved account records. Use the in-app deletion option to also clear saved places, itineraries and queued account feedback on that device. An external web deletion request removes server-side account data; device-only data remains on the device and can be cleared in Settings. Deletion requires an internet connection and password confirmation.

Guest-submitted feedback and guest activity are not tied to a registered account and are not removed by deleting an account. To ask about guest data or this policy, contact the developer using the address below.

Security

Account and feedback requests use HTTPS on the hosted service. Passwords are stored as salted bcrypt hashes. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; keep your password private and contact the developer if you suspect account misuse.

Children and contact

Guide Voyager is a general travel guide and is not designed to collect information from children. For privacy questions or a request concerning guest feedback, contact the app developer at avinashtejdadi@gmail.com.
