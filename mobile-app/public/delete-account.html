GUIDE VOYAGER · ACCOUNT CONTROL

Request account deletion

Sign in with the Guide Voyager account you want to delete. The service will ask for your password and then permanently remove the account and account-linked server data.

This also deletes account-linked activity and feedback. Guest activity and guest feedback are not linked to the account. Saved places, itineraries and preferences are stored only on each device, so they remain there after a web deletion request; open Settings in the app and use “Clear guest data” to remove them. This action cannot be undone.
